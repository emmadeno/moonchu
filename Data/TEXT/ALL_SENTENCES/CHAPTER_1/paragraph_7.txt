On the roof of this little house directly before us, in which John "makee washee, washee," we see squatted three coolies in the characteristic position of the lower classes, not only of China, but of many other Oriental countries.
If the classification of men were made on the same plan as that of birds by ornithologists, these fellows would be styled perchers; for, whether eating, smoking, resting, or in social confab, they are always in this couchant and ungraceful pose.
