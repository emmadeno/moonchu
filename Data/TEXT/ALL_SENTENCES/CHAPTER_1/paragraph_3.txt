Remember we are looking somewhat south of west here.
Then by reference to the maps we can see that the great mass of China lies off to our right, stretching away for over two thousand miles.
Directly before us, six hundred miles distant, is French or Indo-China, and further in that direction is Siam and the Malay Peninsula, Singapore being nearly fifteen hundred miles away.
Luzon, the northernmost of the Philippine Islands, lies over six hundred miles sharply to our left.
Back of us is Formosa, about four hundred miles away, while Tokio, Japan, is one thousand miles beyond Formosa.
San Francisco is nearly six thousand miles distant behind us and over our left shoulder.
Now, with a clearer sense of our location in this part of the earth, we will give further attention to this place immediately before us.
