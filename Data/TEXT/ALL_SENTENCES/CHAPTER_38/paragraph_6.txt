Those mountains are all tea-lands as far as you can see, although there is no tea in sight except a little line of stunted bushes close down on our right.
The tea-plant does not like the vicinity of salt water, but thrives best in mountain air and mountain soil.
These peasants do not seem to differ in appearance from those we have seen in other localities; they wear the queue; their heads are shaven; some are half nude; some wear trousers with shirts outside; some are barefoot and some are shod, and all are impudent in a cowardly fashion.
