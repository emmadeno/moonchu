I think of you, my sovereign ancestors, whose glorious souls are in heaven.
As from an overflowing fountain run the happy streams, such is the connection between you and your descendants.
I, a distant descendant, having received the appointment from heaven, look back and offer this bright sacrifice to you, the honored ones from age to age, for hundreds and thousands and myriads of years.
Now ye front us, O spirits, and now ye pass by us, ascending and descending unrestricted by conditions of space.
Your souls are in heaven; your tablets are in that department.
For myriads of years will your descendants think of you with filial thoughts unwearied.
