Down the river beyond the steamer and before reaching that dark group of buildings we can see several ranges of larger boats extending from mid-stream toward the shore on the left.
Out there we shall see floating dwellings of more beautiful construction.
From those boats called "flower-boats" we shall look toward the city, on our left here.
On the Map No. 4 the red lines connected with the number 4 show the relation between our two positions.
