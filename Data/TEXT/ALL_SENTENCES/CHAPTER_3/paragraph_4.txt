The scene before us is one of the most interesting features of the myriad life of China's greatest commercial city.
As far as our sight can reach we see boats; these boats are homes in which millions of human beings have been born, have lived and have died; and in many cases, without ever having set foot on land.
It has been estimated that in these floating homes from two hundred and fifty to four hundred thousand lives are daily rising and falling with the tide.
