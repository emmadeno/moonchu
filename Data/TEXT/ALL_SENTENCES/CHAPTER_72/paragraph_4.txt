You can see the barricade of brick thrown up on the wall of the bridge; this defensive preparation evidently anticipated the occupation of the wall facing this by the enemy.
We do not here see many traces of shots on the walls of the buildings; this is owing to the circumstance that we are here in the line between fires from opposite directions.
The attacks were chiefly from the east and from the west-from the Ha-ta Gate and beyond it to the east, and from the direction of the Chien Gate and the west and northwest.
Should we go back to the American Legation we would see the west sides of the building riddled with bullets; and should we go eastward till we reached the east sides of the French and German Legations we would find the same condition.
We shall go where we can see each of these Legations in a few minutes.
But first let us glance at those figures nearest us in the caravan.
You can see some French soldiers in the distance, and a couple of American soldiers to our right on the bridge, and the familiar looking telegraph poles and wires which a stranger might be surprised to find in Pekin.
