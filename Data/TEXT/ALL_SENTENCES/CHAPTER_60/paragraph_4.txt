If we turn about and follow the wall westward, only a few paces, past the tower on which we saw the American flag floating, we shall be within the section of the city occupied by the French.
From that point we shall look somewhat east of north over the center of the conquered city.
Our position and field of vision is given on the map by the red lines which start from the south wall, a short distance west of the South Gate, and branch northeast.
The number 61 is given at the starting point of these lines and at their ends on the map margin.
