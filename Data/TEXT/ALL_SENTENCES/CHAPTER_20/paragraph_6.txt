Now that we have taken our first lesson in pigeon English, I will return to my guide the tea-drinker.
On the same day this cadaverous-looking old Cassius accompanied me to the native city where he pretended that certain fees were required, a part of which, I am sure, he kept himself.
Later in the day he engaged to find a native woman who would allow her compressed feet to be photographed; he found one who would thus expose her feet for two dollars, as he said.
I afterward learned that he paid the subject one dollar and kept the other himself.
Thus at this tea-house was my "pigeon squeezed "for the third time in one day.
As we reached the street I entered a rikisha" and said to my hungry old guide: "You squeeze my pigeon three times, I squeeze your pigeon one time," and deducted from his pay three "squeezes," and we parted.
