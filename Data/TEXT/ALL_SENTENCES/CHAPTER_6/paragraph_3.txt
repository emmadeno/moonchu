Down in the little vale below us we can see examples of the care with which the Chinaman cultivates his ground.
Here he is evidently a truck gardener for the great market near at hand; you see how carefully the ground is ridged; how the streamlet from the hillside is carried around the walled compound and along the slope at a proper elevation for irrigating his plot of ground; that it is continued along the base of the hill to his neighbor beyond, where it again does its work of irrigation, and so down the plain in the benevolent perpetuity of Tennyson's "Brook."
