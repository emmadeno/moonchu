After the occupation of the city by the Allies, the American and Japanese troops were placed in charge of the Palace grounds.
A portion of the Ninth Infantry were encamped within this court.
At this time you see them lined along both sides of this Imperial highway as a mark of military respect to the Field Marshal.
You see three mounted officers between a guard on white horses and the escort following.
The central figure, partially hidden by the German flag at the head of the escort, in the rear of the three mounted officers, is the Field Marshal.
He passes through and leaves the court by a gate to the left.
His escort embraces officers from the different armies.
The American Sixth Cavalry lines the way between the gate on which we stand and the next gate behind us toward the Chien-men, and representatives of troops from other nations occupy positions along the route followed.
