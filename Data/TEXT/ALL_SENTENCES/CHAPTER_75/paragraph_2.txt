When we stood on the Ha-ta Gate for our first view in Pekin, we looked westward along the wall to the Chien Gate, which we could see in the distance, about a mile away.
I told you then that later we would visit the latter and stand on the place where Captain Reilly fell.
Now we are on that spot, as nearly as I could have it located, and looking a little north of east and toward the British Legation, which we can see in the middle distance to the right of that tall pole.
