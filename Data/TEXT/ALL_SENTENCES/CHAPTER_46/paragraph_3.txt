I must remind you that Taku is not a stopping place for travellers; it is little more than a pilot station.
All steamers with cargo and passengers for Tien-tsin and Pekin proceed five miles beyond Taku to Tongku.
We shall now follow the narrow, winding Pei-ho to the latter place and from the deck of our steamer at the landing look out upon the ravages of war.
On the Map No. 6 we find our position given by the red lines connected with the number 47.
