Beyond the small house-boat two men in characteristic crouching pose are plainly watching the "foreign devils" and commenting thereon in a foul sarcasm only possible among Chinese.
We see near the same place a woman perched on the roof; we can see another in the distance.
With us "Time is money," with the Chinese time is of little account, but space is money.
