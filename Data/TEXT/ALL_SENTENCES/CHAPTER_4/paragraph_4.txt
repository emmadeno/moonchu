Most travellers and tourists who come to Canton seldom spend more than two or three days in visiting the various places of interest.
Many come on the morning steamer and return to Hongkong by the night boat of the same day.
It is not a desirable place for a long sojourn.
There is so much that is repellent besides the exorbitant prices of poor hotels that a single day may satisfy the sightseer; naturally, therefore, there is a set number of places to be visited in a limited time, and one of these places is the flower-boats.
A question one constantly hears at the hotel is, "Have you been to the flowerboats?"
They have a sort of Monte Carlo notoriety that makes them an object of interest to all travellers.
