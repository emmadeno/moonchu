Now it is a pathetic scene of desolation.
The homeless, starving multitudes have fled.
You see two coolies with wheelbarrows; these have been allowed to pass the guards to gather up scraps of worthless iron, or something of no value from the ruins.
You see also a woman who has been allowed to pass within the gates; we can only conjecture her mission; it is, doubtless, an urgent one, may be, to search for valuable property or missing friends.
Nothing less would tempt her to return at this time.
Besides being a sad picture of a pillaged and deserted city, you can see the character of its architecture; its situation on a level plain; its low one-story brick buildings and narrow streets, this being one of the principal thoroughfares.
You see the gate and tower beyond.
Such gates and towers usually denote the intersection of important streets.
