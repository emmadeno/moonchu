Turning to Map No. 2, the map of Eastern China, we trace the part of our route just before us by the red line which runs from Tien-tsin to Pekin.
A short distance from Tien-tsin we find the number 68 in a circle, both in red, with a zig-zag line running to our route line.
At this place we stop to inspect a line of junk boats on the Pei-ho River.
