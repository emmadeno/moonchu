Coming from Tien-tsin we have approached the wall on the east side near the junction of the Tartar and Chinese cities.
At this place the Tung-pien-men or Tung-pien Gate opens into the Chinese City.
It was at this gate the American forces entered.
We enter here also and pass along within the Chinese City, close to the wall separating it from the Tartar City, until we reach the Ha-ta-men, or Ha-ta Gate, for our first view of the Chinese Capital.
Note the two red lines which start from this gate and branch toward the left or west, each having the number 69 at its end outside the wall.
We are to stand now on the wall, at the point from which these lines start and look west over that part of the city lying between them.
