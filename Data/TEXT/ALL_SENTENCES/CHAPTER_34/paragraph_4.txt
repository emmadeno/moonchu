This Leaning Tower" is an unusually large pagoda; it consists of two walls or a pagoda within a pagoda, each wall being six or seven feet in thickness.
It does not appear so large because our position is several hundred yards away at the foot of the hill, which has an elevation of some fifty feet, the pagoda being nearly two hundred feet in height.
I was obliged to retire to this distance to obtain a view of the entire structure, and even now the first story is not visible.
This venerable old pile suffered more from the Taipings than from thirteen centuries of time.
Formerly the hill was covered with fine temples and palaces before that rebellion, but since then the once magnificent center of "Beautiful Soo" has been a wilderness of ruins.
