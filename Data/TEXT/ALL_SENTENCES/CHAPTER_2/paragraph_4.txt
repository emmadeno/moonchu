A little nearer we see a long, rakish, crouching, demoniacal looking craft with a skulking lowness in the water.
Her appearance betrays her-she is a torpedo boat.
In the center of our field of vision we see a large white ship with three funnels; her lines indicate other purposes than the pursuit of commerce.
She is plainly a warship.
Our field of vision embraces only a narrow space across the channel; throughout its full length there are seldom fewer than fifteen or twenty of these grim arbiters anchored in this focus of Oriental commerce, and they are mostly English.
What a wonderful country is England!
