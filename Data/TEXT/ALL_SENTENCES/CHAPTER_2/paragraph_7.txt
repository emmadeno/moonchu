Those mountains in the distance are rocky and barren as is frequently the case near the sea-coast;
but beyond are many fertile and well-cultivated valleys producing rice, sugar-cane, sweet potatoes, garden fruits and vegetables.
In some portions of this peninsula that curious nut or fruit, sometimes seen in our markets, called the lichee, is abundantly produced.
Along that mountain range to the left, distinctly visible from positions near us, is a long line of excavation that shows the beginning of a railroad that is to connect Hongkong and Canton.
