Within a few feet of us we see some of the vegetation on the mountain-side, and sections of the occasional pinetrees.
My native boy supports himself against one as he also scans the panorama.
I do not now remember whether his back presentation was from choice or necessity, because sometimes the lower classes can be induced to present their backs to the camera when vast sums of money would not induce them to face that dire instrument of evil, believing that when their faces are photographed a part of their identity is forever lost to them, and this becomes a serious matter in their ancestral worship.
