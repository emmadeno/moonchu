Turn with me now to Map No. 6.
This map gives us a sketch of the Pei-ho River from the Pichili Bay to a point about six miles inland, including the sites of the forts at Taku and the town of Tongku.
Find the number 45 in red, inclosed in a circle, and the two red lines which branch from this circle toward the north.
