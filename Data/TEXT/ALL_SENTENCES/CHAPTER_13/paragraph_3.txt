Once I was taken by my guide into a first-class native pharmacy, where the proprietor presented me with a halfdozen small sample bottles of a preparation said to be wonderfully efficacious in curing every form of disease.
I can vouch for its powerful odor, but not for its curative virtues;
I can vouch furthermore for the prohibitive duties put upon it in our own country, for while the six small vials were valueless to me and to everybody, the custom-house appraiser in New York, ignorant of the contents of the tiny bottles, called them medical preparations and taxed me one dollar and fifty cents duty.
Where ignorance is profitable it is folly to be wise.
