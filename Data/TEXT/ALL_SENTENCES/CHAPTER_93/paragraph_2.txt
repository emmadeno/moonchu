We are in the presence of a host of homeless human beings all depending for food and shelter upon the noble little woman seated near the front with a child by her side.
There are men and women, young and old.
All these were sheltered and fed within the English Legation during two of the longest months which any of them have ever experienced.
All within the Legation were saved; but all were not within that nobly defended fortress.
Many friends and kin of these poor people were not so fortunate, but were mercilessly slain by the bloodthirsty Boxers.
