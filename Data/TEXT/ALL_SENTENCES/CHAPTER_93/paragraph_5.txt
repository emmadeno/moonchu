In this same court where these honorary umbrellas stood and where were the groups of Chinese and Manchu ladies, I met Prince Su, whose palace and property were turned over to the beleaguered Legationers; he had proved from the beginning a friend to all the foreigners, and was on the best of terms with the people of the London Mission.
He is a man of fine appearance and seems to delight in mingling with foreigners.
He quite readily granted me a sitting for a stereoscopic photograph, and presented me with his card, explaining at the same time that it is not customary for a Chinese prince to use cards, though on account of his intimate relations with many foreigners he found it convenient to deviate from court usage.
Instead of showing you his picture I give below a VISITING CARD OF PRINCE SU.
