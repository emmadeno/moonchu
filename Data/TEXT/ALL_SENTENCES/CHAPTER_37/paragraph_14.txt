I have spoken of the mandarin as "His Excellency," and of his domicile as the palace; but there did not appear to be any excellent qualities about him or anything palatial about his residence.
He was ordinary in appearance, stupid and boorish in manner, devoid of hospitality and actuated alone by suspicion.
His home was a tileroofed, one-story agglomeration of sheds made of sundried brick without and finished within in the crudest fashion with hand-sawed lumber.
He was plainly a country mandarin.
