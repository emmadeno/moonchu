You see in the distance what appears to be a small pagoda, and beyond a small tower; they both might be called towers of silence; but they are really baby-towers; that is, they are towers in which babes are buried, or rather pitched.
All babes under one year of age at death are wrapped in cloths, bound around with strings, and thrown into these towers.
It may even be suspected that these baby-towers may be the bourne of many girl-babes before death; you know girl infanticide is not uncommon, and here is facile modus.
In illustration of how prevalent is the destruction of girl-babes, one writer tells about seeing the following notice posted at the edge of a pond: Girls may not be drowned here.
