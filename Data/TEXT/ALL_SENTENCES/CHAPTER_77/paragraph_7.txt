Now that we have seen Chien-men Street in the Chinese City and know how it appears, how low and mean-looking the buildings are, how ragged and miserable the common street sellers are, we shall follow this same street directly north to the Chien Gate again.
Thence we shall pass eastward a short distance along Legation Street, thence northward outside the wall of the Imperial City about half its length, until we reach a breach in the wall made by the Allies to facilitate transportation.
This we enter and thereby conveniently gain access to Coal Hill, which we have already seen several times at a distance.
It is about one hundred and fifty feet high, and from its summit we are to obtain fine prospects of the more important parts of the city.
