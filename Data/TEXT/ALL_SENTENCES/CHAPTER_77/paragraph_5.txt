Here again we have before us a typical crowd in this important Chien-men (great street).
While the buildings are used for stores, still the most active merchandizing is carried on in the open air.
Something much trafficked in here is Chinese coal, a mixture of coal dust and clay, and is sold in baskets.
Shoes and other articles of clothing are bought and sold alongside the open air restaurants, where such toothsome dishes as grasshoppers, dog flesh and vegetable mixtures are served to the fastidious but hungry wayfarer who wishes to have some change from rice and tea diet.
