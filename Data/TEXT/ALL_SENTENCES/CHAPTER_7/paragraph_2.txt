We are standing on a foot-bridge that enables night. policemen to pass from roof to roof, and are looking down into Shappat-po Street, one of the principal business streets, especially for merchants who deal in European goods.
Shappat-po Street is a curious sounding name in our ears because it has not been anglicized.
Another street near by called Hog Lane is more intelligible to the Anglo-Saxon, and would scarcely be a misnomer if applied to any of the streets, so narrow and dirty are they all.
