A little farther along we can see another foot-bridge over this street, similar to the one on which we stand.
Policemen nightly patrol these roofs and cross the streets on these bridges.
The buildings are low and the streets are closed by gates or barricades at frequent distances; so that thieves can most readily reach the shops and pass from place to place along the roofs.
A further reason for the bridges is that much industrial work is done on the roofs; clothes are here hung out to dry; frames are erected everywhere for the coloring and drying of cloth and yarn.
You will perceive a halt among those passers below; they have plainly spied the operator, but do not suspect that the eyes of a stereoscopic camera ever look down into that closely sheltered chasm.
