There is a disappointing similarity in the appearance of the people in all parts of China.
They are said to be much larger and stronger in the north, and I presume they are in the average.
But the difference is not very marked.
In the groups we have seen in the south and in the north I scarcely think you have noticed a great difference.
Probably you will say that they seem darker in complexion among the lower classes in the north, and I think they are.
