We have obtained a general view of European Shanghai along the river-front; have looked toward the French settlement, in which the streets have French names.
We have seen the American settlement, where the principal street is called Broadway and the leading hotel the Astor House; and now, as I promised to show you a street back from the river, a street in which, while in the European section, the shops and inhabitants are altogether Chinese, we will leave our elevated view-point over the Custom-house and enter Nankin Road, a cross-street which leads from the Bund through the heart of the English settlement.
